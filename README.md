## 🧭 How I approach analysis

1. **Start with the business question:** who will act on the answer, and what decision does it inform?
2. **Validate the data first:** test accounts, duplicates and broken joins can quietly invalidate conclusions.
3. **Test the tools:** check off-the-shelf models and metrics against real business context.
4. **End with recommendations:** each finding gets an owner, an action and an estimated impact.
